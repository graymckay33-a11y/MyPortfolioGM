About Me

Hi im Gracen

Im a Digital Design student at BTECH, Ive learned how to use Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign as well as skills like Motion Design. I also have experience with Photography and Video Editing, I've had the opportunity to shot engagements, family photos, and even a wedding. I've made 2 short films and several smaller film projects!

My Creative Style

Im drawn to bold Oranges and Blues that pop, I want my designs to chatch peoples eyes and stand out. To me, a successful design is one that grabs your attention and is anything but boring!

Beyond the Screen

When im not designing you'll probably find me behind a camera, learning new hobbies, or out on the ice playing hockey. Im passionate about photography and collecting old cameras, I especially enjoy shooting landscape and nature pieces!
